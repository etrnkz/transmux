Drop-in video player

This page holds no player logic of its own — it mounts one with a single createPlayer() call. AVI, MKV, WMV and FLV are transcoded by FFmpeg.wasm while they play; MP4, MOV and WebM go straight to the browser.

Embedding it somewhere else. The worker and the FFmpeg core are located by URL, so the player also runs from a subdirectory or another origin. Pass both paths explicitly: 
createPlayer({
  container: el,
  workerUrl: '/vendor/wvp/ffmpeg-worker.js',
  coreBaseUrl: '/vendor/wvp/public'
});
The page serving it must send Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy: same-origin and Cross-Origin-Embedder-Policy: require-corp.
